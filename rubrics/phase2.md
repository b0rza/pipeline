# Phase 2 rubric: AI coding-agent enablement

Score a company 0–100. Postings are evidence about the team, not jobs to apply for. Output the score and three one-line reasons.

| Factor | Max | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI tooling named in requirements | 30 | any of claude code, cursor, copilot, codex, windsurf: 20; two or more, or the word "agentic": 30 |
| Pain language | 25 | review bottleneck, velocity, code quality, onboarding, developer experience, platform/DX role: 25; none: 0 |
| Team size proxy | 20 | 3+ open engineering roles, or a DX/platform role: 20; 1–2 roles: 10 |
| Geography and language | 15 | US 15; HR 12; GB, Nordics, Benelux 10; other EU 5 |
| Company type | 10 | agency 10; product company 7; enterprise 3 |

Bands: skip < 40, watch 40–59, draft 60–79, priority ≥ 80.
