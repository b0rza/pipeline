# Phase 1 rubric: contracting (commerce storefront lead)

Score a company 0–100 from its extracted postings. Use the best posting's score as the company score. Output the score and three one-line reasons.

| Factor | Max | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engagement | 25 | contract/freelance 25; unclear 10; perm 0, or 10 if the company is an agency |
| Stack overlap | 25 | Nuxt or Vue 10; a commerce platform we know (SCAYLE, commercetools, SFCC, Shopify Plus) 10; TypeScript 5 |
| Seniority | 15 | senior, lead or architect 15; mid 5; junior 0 |
| Domain | 15 | ecommerce or retail 10; loyalty or payments +5 |
| Geography | 10 | GB, Nordics, Benelux, HR 10; DACH 3; US 0 |
| Urgency | 10 | repost_count ≥ 2 or days_open > 45: 10; days_open > 21: 5 |

Bands: skip < 40, watch 40–59, draft 60–79, priority ≥ 80.

Never raise a score for enthusiasm in the posting text. Only the fields above count.
